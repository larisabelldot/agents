PIP

SCRAP COLLECTOR

A 3D Platformer Adventure

Story

You are PIP, a small robot who collects scrap in the abandoned junkyard. But the junkyard is now overrun by malfunctioning robots! Fight your way through three zones and defeat the guardian boss to restore peace.

Controls

WASD - Move around

SPACE - Jump

SHIFT - Activate Jump Jets (while airborne)

LEFT CLICK - Swing your wrench

Q - Ground Slam (damages nearby enemies)

E - Magnet Pull (attract nearby scrap)

ESC - Pause game

Tips

• Collect scrap to increase your score

• Jump jets regenerate when standing on ground

• Hit enemies with your wrench to destroy them

• Reach checkpoints to save your progress
